The four commands, in order

One verse. Four movements.

The Great Commission isn't a slogan — it's a sequence. Each guide below walks out one part of what Jesus actually told us to do.

IGo

Leave the building

Discipleship starts by going — across the street, across town, or across the world. Carry your faith into ordinary places.

IIMake Disciples

Invite, don't just inform

Faithfulness is measured by lives changed, not information shared. It starts with one relationship at a time.

IIIBaptize

Mark the decision

Baptism is the public, physical yes to following Jesus — a clear line between the old life and the new.

IVTeach

Keep building depth

Discipleship doesn't end at the water. Ongoing teaching roots believers in Scripture for the long run.
